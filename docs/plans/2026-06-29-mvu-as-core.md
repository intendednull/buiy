## Dependencies & risk
- W0→W1→W2→W3→W4 are a strict chain (each builds on the prior). **W5 (perf gate) depends only on W1** — it can run right after the substrate and is the earliest go/no-go signal; scheduled after W4 only to keep the warm-worktree sequence simple, but may be pulled earlier if W1 lands clean.
- **W6 is the highest-risk wave** (the novel AT-seam + the D4 drain-*and*-bind correction + the cross-entity hop). It is gated by the GPU lane + the same-frame tests precisely because the W5-class staleness/clobber bugs are headless-invisible.
- The **go/no-go** is W5: a failure narrows the maximalist framing (per §11) but does **not** kill the substrate/leaf/editor/machine value — record it and proceed.
